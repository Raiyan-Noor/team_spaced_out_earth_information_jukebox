# Use of AI — Earth Jukebox

NASA Space Apps requires every AI tool used in a submission to be named, together with the prompts and the
reasoning. This file is that log. Each entry lists: date, tool, prompt (verbatim when short, otherwise
summarized), what was generated or changed, and what still needs human review.

Humans on the team wrote `CLAUDE.md` (project brief and design decisions) and `docs/SONIFICATION_SPEC.md`
(algorithm spec). Code generated by AI is reviewed by the team before the hackathon.

---

At first the AI stopped because `docs/SONIFICATION_SPEC.md` was missing. The team then added it at the repo root and
replied **"check now"**. The AI moved it into `docs/` and continued.

**Reasoning and decisions the AI made where the spec was ambiguous** (also noted in the commit messages):

- **Demo land mask.** The synthetic planet's land is deliberately concentrated in the northern hemisphere. With a
  symmetric mask, the spec's `sign(lat)` seasonal term cancels in the global mean and the demo melody would be flat.
- **`extreme_frac`.** It needs the 90th percentile over the whole sequence, so it is filled in by a separate
  sequence pass (`features.apply_extreme_fracs`). `frame_features` stays a pure per-frame function.
- **`--range colormap` for MODIS LST.** This uses the inner bounds of the colormap's catch-all end bins (200–350 K).
  The bins are `[0.02,200)` and `[350.02,652)`, and their midpoints (100 K, 501 K) are not physically meaningful.
  Representative values still use the spec's midpoint rule. On the real frames, no pixel falls in those end bins.
- **Pitch details.** Pentatonic quantization breaks ties toward the lower note. The drone pitch is the mean of the
  first 12 frame means (or all frames if there are fewer), sent through the same mapping as the melody.
- **Pan.** For a regional bbox, pan is rescaled across the bbox. For the globe this reduces to the spec's `lon/180`.
- **Default image size.** The default size for non-global regions keeps the bbox's aspect ratio (720 px on the
  longest side), so Bangladesh is not stretched to 2:1.
- **`demo --mode scan`** scans only the July frame of year 1. Scanning all 24 frames would be about 2.5 minutes long.
- **Scan mode details.** Notes use brightness 0.5, since the spec gives scan mode no brightness stream. Column
  coverage (`col_valid`) is weighted by cos(latitude).
- **Additions beyond the spec's CLI.** `--sample-rate` and `--mono` (to fit the samples under 2 MB), `--offline` and
  `--cache-dir` for `gibs`, and `--units` for `image`. Also a module `jukebox/plot.py` for `--plot`, which keeps the
  lazy matplotlib import out of the CLI.
- **Not done from spec P2:** the "diverging-colormap timbre split for anomaly layers". It was not in this session's
  P2 list.

**What was generated or changed:**

- The `jukebox/` package (colormap, decode, features, mapping, score, synth, legend, demo, gibs, cli, plot).
- `tests/` (65 tests; sockets are blocked during tests).
- `samples/`: three WAVs plus their legend and score files.
- `README.md`, `LICENSE`, the requirements files, `.gitignore` and `pyproject.toml`.
- The spec file was moved to `docs/`.

**Verification the AI ran:**

- `pytest -q` passed with 65 tests.
- A fresh clone into a new virtual environment passed install, `pytest` and `demo`.
- `demo` and `gibs` were run. Programmatic checks showed MIDI never decreases as the mean increases, ticks fall on
  January frames, and values are 9.8–19.6 °C global land daytime means.
- An FFT check confirmed that all 36 rendered notes match the score's pitches.
- The live GIBS API was used once to download the capabilities document, the colormap and 36 monthly frames. All
  later runs used the cache. The July 2024 frame had 217 distinct opaque colours, all exact matches to the colormap.
